Delete your account

Request permanent deletion of your AllSplit account and data.

You can request permanent deletion of your AllSplit account and associated personal data. This page explains what is deleted and how to request it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email mobiledejitaru@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: AllSplit Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the mobile number registered in the app
  4. Optionally include your device model and approximate registration date

We will verify ownership of the account (for example by confirming OTP access or other identity checks) and process the request.

What we delete

  • Your user profile (mobile number, name)
  • Device binding / PIN credentials for your account
  • Saved contacts associated with your account
  • Splits you created, where feasible without breaking other users’ records

What may be retained

  • Limited security/audit logs for a short period (fraud prevention / legal compliance)
  • Split records where you are only a participant and another user is the creator (your phone/name reference may be anonymized where possible)

Timeline

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request. You will receive a confirmation email when done (if an email thread was used).

In-app steps before requesting

  • Open AllSplit → Settings → Log out / Switch account if needed
  • Note your registered mobile number for the deletion email
