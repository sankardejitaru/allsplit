Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AllSplit (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the AllSplit mobile application and related services at allsplit.money.

AllSplit is a bill-splitting tool. It helps users create shared expenses, assign amounts, and track settlements. It is not a bank and does not process card payments or move money between bank accounts.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account and profile

  • Mobile phone number (for OTP login and account identification)
  • First name and last name (optional profile details you provide)
  • Device identifier (to bind secure PIN login to your device)
  • Authentication tokens and a hashed PIN (we do not store your PIN in plain text)

1.2 Contacts

  • With your permission, AllSplit may read device contacts so you can add people to a split.
  • Contacts you choose to save in the app may be stored on our servers to help you reuse them.

1.3 Photos and camera

  • With your permission, camera or gallery access is used to scan or upload bill images.
  • Images may be processed by our servers (and, where configured, cloud OCR providers) to extract bill items.

1.4 Split and settlement data

  • Split names, items, amounts, participants, and settlement status (open, pending, received)
  • Activity needed to show “who owes what” and “amounts collected”

1.5 Technical and security logs

  • App/API request metadata and audit events (for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention)

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Send one-time passwords (OTP) for verification
  • Provide bill splitting, tracking, and settlement features
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud, and secure the service
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Permissions

Depending on your device, AllSplit may request:

  • Internet — to sync with our API
  • Contacts — to add people to splits
  • Camera / photos — to scan or upload bills

You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

4. Sharing of information

We may share data only as needed to operate the service, for example:

  • Other participants in a split you create or join (names, phones, owed amounts related to that split)
  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, databases, SMS/WhatsApp OTP delivery if enabled, OCR processing if enabled)
  • Authorities when required by law

5. Data storage and security

  • Data is transmitted using HTTPS where supported.
  • Account credentials such as PIN are stored hashed.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Data retention

We retain account and split data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion as described below.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile name in the app Settings
  • Log out or switch account on your device
  • Request access to or deletion of your account data (see Delete Account)

8. Children’s privacy

AllSplit is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

If you use AllSplit from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be processed in that country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
